Welcome to Space Lab

Build your own mini-universe and experiment with gravity, collisions, black holes and light.

  • Move camera: drag empty space. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
  • Move an object: choose Select, then drag the object.
  • Create: choose Planet, Star, Black Hole, Rock or Light, then tap or drag on the space.
  • Launch: when creating an object, drag away from its starting point before releasing.
  • Change mass: tap an object and type a number in its Mass box. Values like 1e12 work.
  • Zoom: pinch, use the View button to reset, or use the + / − buttons below.

Tip: start with the Solar System or Black Hole preset. Turn light rays on only when you need them; they are designed to update less often to reduce lag.
